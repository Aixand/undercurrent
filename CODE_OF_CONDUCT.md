# Code of conduct

Undercurrent welcomes participation regardless of experience, background,
identity, or ability. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to
help maintain a respectful environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and open to feedback.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Respect different perspectives, personal boundaries, and privacy.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help resolve their effects.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information
without permission are unacceptable. Retaliation against anyone raising a
concern in good faith is also unacceptable.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, other
project-managed spaces, and participation while officially representing the
project. Technical disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to
[corporate@aixand.org](mailto:corporate@aixand.org), using the subject
`Undercurrent conduct report`. The project is maintained by Artificial
Intelligence Advancement Initiative Inc. Include relevant links or details
and how you would like to be contacted. Avoid public accusations or sharing
unrelated private information.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to
investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. A
maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome; identify any
conflict of interest when reporting so an uninvolved representative can review
the concern.

Responses may include asking for a correction, removing content, issuing a
warning, restricting participation temporarily, or banning participation.
Maintainers will consider the behavior, its impact, and any repeated conduct,
and explain their decision to the people affected where appropriate. Immediate
restrictions may be necessary to stop ongoing harm.

You may request reconsideration at the same address with relevant additional
information and ask for review by an uninvolved representative. This policy
governs participation in project spaces; it does not change the software's
license or transfer anyone's intellectual property rights.
